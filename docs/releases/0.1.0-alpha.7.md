## Updating

IPAForge 0.1.0-alpha.6 updates itself to this version when it opens: your Apple IDs, apps and settings stay.

## What's new

- **Developer Portal** (it replaces Certificates in the sidebar): everything your Apple ID's team holds at Apple, in tabs. Certificates, with who made each and which apps on your iPhone it signs; App IDs, with the app using each and how many are left this week; the provisioning profiles on your iPhone; app groups; and registered devices. Revoke a certificate, delete an App ID nothing uses, or remove expired and unused profiles, each after a confirmation that says what changes.
- **Pairing files for every app:** **Pairing files** on the Apps page shows whose pairing file each app holds (SideStore's remote one too). Place one, place it again or remove it, give one to an app of your own at the path its instructions name, or make a new remote pairing for SideStore. An app that needs one says so on its row. On the Devices page, **Save pairing file…** keeps the device's pairing file for another tool.
- **Save signed .ipa instead:** tick it on an app in Preload, and IPAForge signs it with your Apple ID and saves the `.ipa` in a folder you choose, instead of installing it. Saved files never contain your certificate's private key.
- **Your certificate as a .p12:** export the one this computer signs with, under a password, for SideStore or another computer; or import one from them, so this computer signs with it too, with no new certificate and no slot used.
- **Patreon:** you can now support IPAForge on [Patreon](https://patreon.com/IPAForge) as well as GitHub Sponsors.

## Changed

- **Cancel refresh** on the Apps page while a refresh or update runs.

## Before you start

- **macOS:**
  - IPAForge isn't notarised by Apple yet. Open it once, then go to *System Settings › Privacy & Security* and click **Open Anyway**.
  - When asked about *IPAForge Safe Storage*, click **Always Allow**.
- **Windows:**
  - SmartScreen warns about the download: click *More info › Run anyway*.
  - Install *Apple Devices* from the Microsoft Store first.
- **Linux:** install `usbmuxd`.
- **Your device:** iOS 15 or later with a passcode; on iOS 16 and later, Developer Mode must be on.

To check a download or the installed app, and for every step, see the [installation guide](https://github.com/IPAForge/ipaforge/blob/main/docs/installing.md).
