Anisette servers you can choose, sign-in that asks less, a Refresh that keeps your apps as they are, and SideStore's remote pairing for iOS 17.4 and later. Please report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/IPAForge/ipaforge/issues), with the logs attached (**Logs**, ⌘L / Ctrl+L), or ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/C87DKmAWhr).

## Updating

IPAForge 0.1.0-alpha.5 updates itself to this version when it opens: your Apple IDs, apps and settings stay. On a Mac with Apple silicon the update is the Apple silicon build, even if you installed the Intel one.

## What's new

- **Anisette servers in Settings:** IPAForge picks a working server by itself (the servers you added, SideStore's list, then its own as a backup), or you choose one. Test one or all, update SideStore's list, and add your own, a server on your computer included.
- **Remote pairing for SideStore (iOS 17.4 and later):** placing the pairing file now also gives SideStore its remote pairing, in a file of its own, and Repair SideStore checks it. SideStore builds from September 20, 2026 can use it.
- **The app's mark** next to its name at the top.

## Changed

- **Refresh keeps your apps as they are:** it never installs another version (that's Update's job). SideStore is installed again over itself, its data kept, so its own screen shows the new date; other apps get a new profile only.
- **Days left are counted as SideStore counts them:** a fresh profile says 7 days, not 6.
- **Sign in again** uses your saved password and asks only for the code. Checks in the background never ask Apple to send you a code.
- **On launch** only the update check runs; your devices and Apple IDs come after.

## Fixed

- After you refuse the keychain prompt once, IPAForge asks again next time, so your Apple IDs are saved once you allow it.
- The Intel build running on an Apple silicon Mac says so and links to the faster Apple silicon build.
- Testing an anisette server asks it once, so a server that's down shows "Not responding" at once.

You can ignore the `.zip`, `.blockmap` and `latest*.yml` files: they are for automatic updates. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and every file has a Sigstore signature (`<file>.sigstore.json`), which IPAForge also checks before installing an update.

## Before you start

- **macOS:**
  - IPAForge isn't notarised by Apple yet. Open it once, then go to *System Settings › Privacy & Security* and click **Open Anyway**.
  - When asked about *IPAForge Safe Storage*, click **Always Allow**.
- **Windows:**
  - SmartScreen warns about the download: click *More info › Run anyway*.
  - Install *Apple Devices* from the Microsoft Store first.
- **Linux:** install `usbmuxd`.
- **Your device:** iOS 15 or later with a passcode; on iOS 16 and later, Developer Mode must be on.

To check a download or the installed app, and for every step, see the [installation guide](https://github.com/IPAForge/ipaforge/blob/main/docs/installing.md).
