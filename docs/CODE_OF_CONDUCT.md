# Code of Conduct

Loadstar adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its code of conduct. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space is expected to follow it.

In short:

- **Be respectful** and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- **Assume good faith.** Give constructive feedback on the work, not the person.
- **No harassment.** That includes insulting or demeaning comments, unwelcome attention, and publishing others' private information (including Apple IDs, device identifiers or pairing files).
- **Stay on topic.** Support requests for piracy or for bypassing Apple's protections will be closed.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@SirReaDy](https://github.com/SirReaDy). Use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, issue or pull request, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct. Depending on severity, they may also warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a participant, as described in the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant.
